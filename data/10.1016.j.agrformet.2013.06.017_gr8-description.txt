The figure shows two maps of Australia illustrating the spatial elasticity of gross primary productivity (GPP) to precipitation (left) and to temperature (right), each with its own colour scale ranging from low (yellow/green) to high (blue/black) sensitivity. Beneath the maps, corresponding scatter plots of mean annual temperature versus precipitation display individual points coloured by the same elasticity values, revealing how sensitivity varies across climatic gradients. Overall, GPP generally increases more with precipitation in wetter–cooler regions, while its response to temperature is mostly negative and strongest in warmer–drier areas.
