The figure compares two spatial maps of Australia that use color gradients to depict the elasticity of (left) vegetation transpiration and (right) soil evaporation with respect to precipitation, where yellow indicates low elasticity and dark blue to black represents high elasticity. Beneath each map, a scatter plot shows annual precipitation (y-axis) versus mean temperature (x-axis) for multiple locations, with points colored by the same elasticity scale as the corresponding map. Together, the panels illustrate how the sensitivity of water fluxes to rainfall varies geographically and across climate space.
