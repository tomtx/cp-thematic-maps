The three side-by-side maps show recent decade-scale trends across South America in precipitation (left), maximum temperature (center) and minimum temperature (right), each with its own diverging color bar. Blues on the precipitation panel indicate widespread decreases—especially over central and southern Brazil—while reds on the temperature panels reveal broad warming of daily highs and lows, most strongly in eastern and southeastern Brazil. A boxed rectangle highlights a focus region in southern Brazil, Paraguay and northern Argentina.
