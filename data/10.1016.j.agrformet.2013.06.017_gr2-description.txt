The composite figure shows four color-coded maps of Australia depicting annual precipitation, mean temperature, evapotranspiration and gross primary production. Each map uses a gradient scale (e.g., precipitation from yellow to dark blue and temperature from black through red to yellow) to illustrate strong spatial contrasts, with wetter, cooler and more productive areas concentrated along northern and eastern coasts and drier, hotter, less productive conditions dominating the continent’s interior. Legends beneath each panel provide units: precipitation and evapotranspiration in millimetres per year, temperature in degrees Celsius, and gross primary production in kilograms of carbon per square metre per year.
