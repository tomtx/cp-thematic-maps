The figure presents two maps of Australia, coloured to show the spatial distribution of the temperature elasticity of transpiration (left) and of soil evaporation (right), with warmer colours indicating higher positive elasticity and darker shades indicating negative or lower values. Below each map, a corresponding scatter plot depicts precipitation versus mean temperature for numerous locations, using the same colour scale to relate each point to its mapped elasticity value. Together, the panels illustrate how sensitivity to temperature varies geographically and with climate conditions across the continent.
