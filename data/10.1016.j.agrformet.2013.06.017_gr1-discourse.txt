For regional assessment, the Australian continent is partitioned as in previous work (Haverd et al., 2013a) into six regions (Fig. 1 ) with internally similar climate and biophysical characteristics: tropics, savanna, warm temperate, cool temperate, Mediterranean, and desert. Fig. 3 shows (for the regions in Fig. 1 and the whole of Australia) the elasticities of ET, transpiration and soil evaporation to six drivers V j (P, T, C A , S, U and L). For all Australian regions (Fig. 1) the runoff–rainfall ratio P/Q is significantly greater than 1, so the first of Eq. (10) implies that ?(Q, V j ) is of opposite sign to ?(E, V j ) for drivers T, C A , S, U and L. Noting that ?(E, P) is positive and less than 1 (Fig. 3), the second of Eq. (10) implies that ?(Q, P) is positive and a little less than P/Q.
